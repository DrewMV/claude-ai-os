# Cyber Risk Register — How to Submit a Risk

## Summary

To report a risk to the PPL Cyber Risk Register, submit a **Risk Identification Form** in OneTrust. Anyone can submit the form — you do not need to be on the Cyber Risk team. Once submitted, the Cyber Risk team owns the process from that point forward: they evaluate the risk, engage stakeholders, and determine next steps.

## Steps

1. Open the **OneTrust Self-Service Portal**: https://app.onetrust.com/ssp/assessments/list
2. Select **Risk Identification Form** from the list of available forms.
3. Complete the requested information describing the risk and submit.
4. The **Cyber Risk team** reviews the submission, engages appropriate stakeholders, gathers additional details as needed, and determines next steps.

## Key Points

- **Anyone can submit** — no special role or permission required.
- **You are not responsible for evaluating the risk** — the form is for identification only; the Cyber Risk team handles assessment and advancement.
- **Use this when** a discovered gap, compliance issue, or operational exposure warrants formal tracking outside the team's backlog.
